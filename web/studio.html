Fig lives in r4box. r4box is Fig's mind: the chaos curve, x → r·x·(1−x), is its mood and the games are what it dreams. Fig goes with everyone, and it has four personalities, one for each pillar of the box, that the curve brings out as you play: a peak makes it wild, a mirror makes it mirror, the window boxes it up, a golden cut turns it golden. Each one bends the curve its way and pays double for its own kind of beat. Here they are side by side, held in place so you can poke them; in a game, Fig moves between them on its own.
